## Ace SRV 0.1.0

First port of DiscordSRV to Pumpkin.

- Two-way chat between Minecraft and Discord, with role colours, aliases, replies, mentions, emotes and attachments
- Join, first join, leave, death and advancement embeds, with optional webhooks showing player heads
- Server start and stop messages, live channel topics and a rotating bot status
- Console channel and `!c` commands, `playerlist`, canned responses
- Account linking by DM code, with a Linked role and link/unlink commands
- `/discord` with invite, link, unlink, linked, reload and broadcast
